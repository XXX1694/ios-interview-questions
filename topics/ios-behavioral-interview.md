# Behavioral Interview interview questions

App Store rejections, WWDC and Swift 6 migrations, UIKit to SwiftUI, crash triage, HIG pushback, phased-release mistakes, coaching juniors. Junior · 17 questions.

**[Practice this topic on irbisa.com →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/)** · [All iOS topics](../README.md#all-topics)

### 1. Walk me through your iOS background: which apps have you shipped, and what do you do in Xcode day to day?

Open with the **app you ship now** and your role in it, add the one earlier step that explains your path, and close with why this iOS team fits what you want next.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76401)

### 2. What is making you look beyond your current iOS job, and why did this company catch your eye?

Give one **honest reason** phrased as what you are moving toward, then connect it to something concrete you noticed in this company's iOS product.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76402)

### 3. Tell me about an iOS feature you shipped that you are proud of. What did you own?

Choose a feature that used **a platform capability** well, such as a widget, a Live Activity or App Intents, and explain why it mattered to users, what you decided and what moved afterwards.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76403)

### 4. Which iOS skills would your teammates name as your strongest, and which area do you still avoid?

Answer the way **your teammates would**: two strengths they rely on you for, one area you still hand to someone else, and what you are doing to stop handing it over.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76404)

### 5. What compensation do you expect for this iOS position?

Quote a **range from research**, not from your current pay, and make clear whether it is gross per year or net per month and that the final figure hinges on the whole package.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76405)

### 6. Do you have any questions about our iOS team or the way we ship?

Use the time to learn **how the iOS app is built and released**, and pick questions this particular interviewer can actually answer: an engineer on code, a manager on priorities.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76406)

### 7. Tell me about the toughest iOS crash you have debugged. How did you find the cause?

Pick a crash that **never happened on your own device**, and show how crash reports, Instruments and a sanitizer led you to the cause and what now stops it from returning.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76407)

### 8. Tell me about an iOS release where your change hurt users. What did you do, and what do you do differently now?

Pick a **real mistake** of yours with limited damage, explain how you contained it given that App Store builds cannot be rolled back, and end on the safeguard you added.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76408)

### 9. Tell me about an App Store rejection that landed right before a launch. How did you handle it?

Show that you **read the cited guideline** before reacting, warned stakeholders the same hour, chose one path (fix, reply or appeal) with an owner, and changed the submission checklist afterwards.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76409)

### 10. You and another iOS developer disagreed about the app's architecture. How was it settled?

Describe an architecture argument where you **traded opinions for criteria** both sides accepted, how it was settled, and what the working relationship looked like afterwards.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76410)

### 11. Your feature needs a new entitlement and a pass through App Review. How do you size it, and what happens when the date is at risk?

Estimate the code and **the path to the App Store** separately, since review, entitlements and TestFlight rounds add days, and warn the PM the moment either part slips.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76411)

### 12. A designer hands you a custom control that goes against Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. What do you do?

Treat the HIG as **evidence, not a rulebook**: show the designer what the custom control costs in accessibility and review risk, offer a styled system control that keeps the look, and build what is finally decided.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76412)

### 13. How do you take criticism in code review, and how do you point out a problem in someone else's Swift code?

Show you can **hear criticism without defending yourself** and raise problems by describing what the code does and what that causes, with one story where feedback changed your habits.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76413)

### 14. How does your team handle each WWDC and the iOS release that follows in September?

Describe a **yearly routine**: sessions split across the team in June, the app built on the Xcode beta over the summer, required changes planned with product before September, and one release you prepared that way.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76414)

### 15. Tell me about a junior iOS developer you coached to independence. What did you do, and what changed for the team?

Show that you **teach by doing, then automate**: one person's recurring mistake, the sessions where you worked through it together, and the check you added so the whole team stops making it.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76415)

### 16. How did you convince product to fund a Swift 6 strict-concurrency migration or a similar piece of iOS tech debt?

Tie the work to **something Apple or users will force anyway**, such as a new SDK rule, a crash or a review risk, so the question becomes when to pay rather than whether, and offer the cheapest schedule.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76416)

### 17. You led a UIKit to SwiftUI migration. How did you plan it and get the team on board?

Show a migration **led through people**: a reason the business accepted, a small pilot with numbers, shared rules for mixing UIKit and SwiftUI, and features shipping the whole time.

[Full answer →](https://irbisa.com/topic/ios-behavioral-interview/#q-76417)
